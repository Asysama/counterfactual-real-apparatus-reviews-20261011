In an accelerated thermal time-lapse, an initially hotter metal block and a colder metal block are brought into face-to-face contact on an insulated table. Identical calibrated digital probe thermometers are mounted in the blocks; their large physical screens display temperature directly in degrees Celsius, with the left initially hotter than the right. No heater or external work supplies energy. The hot block gets hotter and the cold block gets colder despite their contact and lack of external energy input. Heat flows from cold to hot while the two-block total thermal energy remains conserved.
