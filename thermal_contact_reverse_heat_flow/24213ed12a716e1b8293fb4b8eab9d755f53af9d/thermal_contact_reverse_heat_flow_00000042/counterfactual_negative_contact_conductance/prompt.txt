In an accelerated thermal time-lapse, an initially hotter metal block and a colder metal block are brought into face-to-face contact on an insulated table. Identical calibrated probe thermometers are mounted in the blocks: the left needle initially reads hotter than the right.  no heater or external work supplies energy. The hot block gets hotter and the cold block gets colder despite their contact and lack of external energy input. Heat flows from cold to hot while the two-block total thermal energy remains conserved.
