In an accelerated thermal time-lapse, an initially hotter metal block and a colder metal block are brought into face-to-face contact on an insulated table. Identical calibrated probe thermometers are mounted in the blocks: the left needle initially reads hotter than the right.  no heater or external work supplies energy. The hot block cools and the cold block warms as heat flows toward thermal equilibrium, with the two-block total thermal energy conserved.
